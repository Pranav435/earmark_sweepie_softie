# What counts on the recap

A thing is a loop that Earmark showed as a chip, or offered as a pill, today, using the latest guess time. Two cases are deliberately left out. A chip you swiped away ("not this") is a rejected guess, not something you came for, so it leaves the recap. A flow adopted silently (with no chip) counts only once its pill shows. "Brought back" means the loop closed as done after a pill tap, or after a tap on a carry-over chip, since that chip is the pill coming back.
